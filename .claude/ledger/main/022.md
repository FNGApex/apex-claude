## Goal

From: 021

`claude plugin` calls in install/uninstall act on the config dir the installer was given (-ConfigDir, CLAUDE_DIR), not
always the real one.

## Approach

  token-spend@apex, usage-footer@apex. Makefile path not run (no make on this machine).
